Where It Began

Born From a
Love of Film

CineLensStudios was founded in 2018 by Aryan Mehta, a self-taught photographer who fell in love with the language of cinema long before he ever picked up a camera.

Growing up watching the way a single frame in a film could hold an entire lifetime of emotion, Aryan became obsessed with bringing that same deliberateness to still photography. Every composition, every light decision, every moment of stillness — intentional.

What started as a one-person passion project in a Mumbai apartment has grown into a team of six storytellers, covering everything from grand destination weddings to quiet executive portraits.

"I never wanted to be a photographer who just showed up with a camera. I wanted to be a storyteller who happened to use one."

— Aryan Mehta, Founder
How We Think

Our Philosophy

Three principles that guide every shoot, every edit, every delivery we make.

01

Presence Over Performance

We don't direct people into poses. We create an environment where genuine moments emerge on their own. The camera finds you — not the other way around.

02

Light as Language

Every shoot is a study in available light. We chase golden hours, lean into shadows, and treat the sun as a collaborator. Artificial lighting is a last resort, never a default.

03

Story First, Always

Before we touch the shutter, we understand the narrative. What chapter of your life is this? What should someone feel when they look at this frame in thirty years?
